Data & Science

  • NASA DONKI (space weather): real solar flares and CMEs set the radiation risk each day. The badge at the top shows if the events are live from NASA, or a saved sample.
  • NASA OSDR (space biology): the science facts on each result screen.
  • Simplified game model: damage numbers, decay and the 20-unit budget are game rules, not real dose measurements. Real habitats are far more complex.
  • Lunar night: the Moon has about 14 Earth days of darkness, so solar power drops. In this game it lasts one day.
